Privacy Policy: Moonlit Grove Icons

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Moonlit Grove Icons is an icon pack and wallpaper app. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • It does not send any data from your phone to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

  • The app can set a wallpaper on your phone when you ask it to. It does this on the phone itself.
  • To show which of your apps already have an icon, the app reads the list of installed apps on your phone. That list never leaves your phone.

Icon requests

If you request an icon, the app opens your own email app with a message already written (the names of the apps you chose). Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use emailed requests only to decide which icons to make next, and we don't share them.

Purchases

The app is bought through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your copy was bought, and nothing else.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
